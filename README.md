# History through coins

*Kingdoms swell and vanish, borders shift like tides, and emperors fall to the knife. Most of what they built is dust now, but their gold still turns up in the ground, as bright as the day it was struck.*

A map of the ancient world from 650 BC to AD 1204, told through its gold coins. Slide through the years and watch the borders change. Wars flare up and burn out, mints open and close, and every coin sits where it was struck.

**[Open the map →](https://escargot-studio.github.io/history-through-coins/)**  ·  Companion to the [Gold Coin Catalogue](https://escargot-studio.github.io/gold-coin-catalogue/ancient.html), where every coin has its own card.

## What's inside

- **Borders by year**, from archaic Greece to the sack of Constantinople, with today's borders as a faint guide.
- **Gold coins on their mints**: click one for both sides, the ruler and the story.
- **Wars and battles**: each war's sides and battles appear while the slider is in its years.
- **Stories**: short illustrated walks, such as the owl of Athens, Alexander's gold after Alexander, and Caesar's rise and fall.
- **Learn**: gentle lessons on how money began and why gold mattered.
